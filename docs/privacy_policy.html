VeoPlay – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026 · Developer: Akhlakh Ansari · Contact: [YOUR-EMAIL@example.com]

1. Summary

VeoPlay is a video and music player. It works on your device. We do not collect, upload, sell or share your personal information.

2. What the app stores on your device

The following is saved only on your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else: the name you enter (for display), favorites, playlists, watch history, playback positions, bookmarks, app settings, and a list of your video files (names and locations on your phone) that is kept to open the app faster.

3. Permissions and why we need them

  • Photos/Videos and Music/Audio (Media permission): to find and play the videos and songs stored on your phone.
  • Notifications: to show music controls (play, pause, next, favorite) in the notification and on the lock screen.
  • Internet: only to play a video link that you type or paste yourself (network stream).
  • Foreground service / Wake lock: to keep music playing when the screen is off and to keep the screen on during video.
  • Storage (older Android versions): same purpose as Media permission on older phones.

4. Network links you play

If you enter a video link, the app connects directly to that website or server to play it. That server may see your IP address, as with any website or video app. VeoPlay does not control or receive that information.

5. Third parties

VeoPlay currently contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services. If we add any in the future, this policy will be updated first.

6. Children

VeoPlay is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.

7. Deleting your data

Open Settings → Clear All Data in the app, or uninstall the app. All data stored by VeoPlay is removed from your device. Your own video and music files are never touched.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
